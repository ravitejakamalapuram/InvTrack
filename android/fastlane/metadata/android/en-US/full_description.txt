Track the investments your broker app can't see: fixed deposits, P2P lending, bonds, chit funds and gold. Record money in and out, and see your real return (XIRR).

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK
• Fixed deposits (FDs)
• P2P lending
• Bonds, NCDs and debentures
• Chit funds
• Gold, including Sovereign Gold Bonds
• Real estate
• Invoice discounting
• Private equity and angel investments
• Crypto
• Anything else where money goes in and comes back later

InvTrack is a ledger, not a price feed. You record what you put in and what came back (returns, interest, fees). It does not fetch market prices.

REAL RETURNS, WORKED OUT FOR YOU
A simple percentage ignores time. InvTrack uses XIRR, the annual-return formula behind Excel's XIRR, so the date of every payment counts.

Example: you put ₹1,00,000 into an FD on 1 April 2025 and get ₹1,07,100 back on 1 April 2026. That is 7.1% a year. Get the same ₹1,07,100 back on 1 October 2025 instead, and it is about 14.7% a year, because your money did the same work in half the time.

You also see total invested, total returned, net profit and how many times your money has multiplied (MOIC).

MADE FOR INDIA, WORKS GLOBALLY

DATA IN AND OUT
• Add many investments at once from a CSV file, using the template in the app
• Export your whole portfolio as a ZIP file of CSVs, and import it on another device
• Attach PDFs and photos, such as FD receipts and statements, to an investment

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
• Your data is stored in your InvTrack account on Google Firebase. It is private to your account and never sold.
• No ads.
• Attached documents stay on your phone.
• Lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint.
• Privacy mode hides amounts on screen with one tap.
• Works offline after first sign-in. Changes sync when you are back online.
• Delete your account and data any time from Settings.

WHAT INVTRACK IS NOT
• Not a broker. You cannot buy or sell anything in the app.
• Not an investment adviser. It shows your numbers; it does not tell you what to buy.
• No bank or SMS access. InvTrack does not read your bank account or your messages. You enter your own data.
